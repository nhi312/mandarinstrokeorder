Which textbook are you following?你用的是哪一本課本？

These share the same English name but are different books with different characters and content. Pick the one on your desk — the big glyphs below are how each book actually prints its characters: bold traditional next to bold simplified. You can switch at any time from the toolbar.

The two NTNU books' vocabulary was extracted directly from the textbook's own 生詞索引 pages. Each word's lesson number is read from the PDF's own text layer (not OCR, which was dropping leading digits and collapsing Lesson 11 into Lesson 1), and every character is then cross-checked twice: against a Chinese-English dictionary, and against the pinyin printed next to it in the index. A row is only kept when all of that agrees — so Book 1 has 373 words and Book 2 has 326, out of roughly 500–600 per book. The rest were dropped rather than guessed at: coverage is partial, but what's here is verified. Vietnamese meanings are included for every word. English glosses come from the dictionary and occasionally show a less common sense of a word; flag anything that looks off. The Wu Zhongwei book remains a reconstruction from memory.
