Which textbook are you following?你用的是哪一本課本？

These share the same English name but are different books with different characters and content. Pick the one on your desk — the big glyphs below are how each book actually prints its characters: bold traditional next to bold simplified. You can switch at any time from the toolbar.

The two NTNU books' vocabulary was extracted directly from the textbook's own 生詞索引 (vocabulary index) pages and checked against a Chinese-English dictionary, so the characters, readings, and lesson placement are the real thing — not a reconstruction. Coverage is partial: about 400–430 of each book's roughly 500–600 words came through clean; the rest were dropped rather than guessed at, so nothing on the page is invented. English glosses come from that same dictionary and occasionally show a less common sense of a word; flag anything that looks off. The Wu Zhongwei book is a reconstruction from memory, as before.
